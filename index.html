How your shipping rate is calculatedWeight, dimensions and zone assumptions.
  • Enter weight in oz, lb or kg. 1 lb = 16 oz = 0.45359237 kg. Unit switches preserve actual weight.
  • Dimensional weight: L × W × H (in) ÷ 166 in lb, or L × W × H (cm) ÷ 6,000 in kg. Both formulas come from the supplied PDF and are not exactly equivalent; switching dimension units can change the result.
  • The greater of actual and dimensional weight is converted to kg and matched against the system export’s exact inclusive start/end ranges. No unprovided rounding rule is applied. Weights between exported bands require confirmation.
  • Choose a zone confirmed by DSCP. No ZIP-to-zone mapping was supplied. When the zone is unknown, the range covers Zones 1–8.
  • Shipping rates only. Final eligibility and any applicable surcharges require confirmation.
